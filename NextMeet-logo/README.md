# NextMeet logo

Two hearts lean into each other: pink is you, violet is your partner. A small gap keeps both hearts whole.

## Files
**svg/** (use these first, they scale to any size; text is converted to outlines)
- `logo-horizontal.svg`: main logo (mark and wordmark side by side)
- `logo-stacked.svg`: mark above the wordmark
- `logo-mark.svg`: the two hearts only
- `logo-wordmark.svg`: "NextMeet" only
- `logo-*-reverse-on-pink.svg`: for the pink brand background
- `logo-*-mono-ink.svg` and `logo-*-mono-white.svg`: one-colour versions
- `app-icon.svg` (square, no rounded corners: give this to the App Store and Play Store), `app-icon-rounded.svg`, `app-icon-light.svg`, `app-icon-violet.svg`, `app-icon-mascots.svg`, `favicon.svg`

**png/** ready-made exports with transparent backgrounds (app icons 1024, 512 and 180, logo 1600 to 2400 wide, favicon 64 and 32).

## Colours
Pink #D81B6A, Violet #7B5CF0, Ink #2B1B26, Butter #FFD66B, Blush #FFF4F8. On pink backgrounds use the lighter hearts #FFB3D1 and #C9B8FF.

## Rules
- Keep clear space equal to the height of the N on every side.
- Minimum width: 96 px for the full logo, 28 px for the mark, 29 px for the app icon.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolour or place it on a clashing colour.
- Wordmark font: Nunito ExtraBold (already outlined in the files).
